My Approach

Small habits.
Consistent progress.

I understand that academic success is not only about studying before an examination. It also depends on how consistently I organise my time, participate in learning and take responsibility for my development.

My goal is to become a student who understands what I learn and knows how to apply it.
01

Plan my week

I will list lectures, assignment deadlines and revision sessions at the beginning of each week so that I can manage my time responsibly.

02

Review my notes

I will revisit lecture notes regularly, summarise important ideas in my own words and identify topics that need more attention.

03

Practise practical skills

I will set aside time to practise Word, Excel and PowerPoint, gradually developing confidence with the tools used in academic and professional environments.

04

Ask questions and collaborate

When I do not understand something, I will seek clarification from lecturers or classmates and participate constructively in group learning.

05

Reflect and improve

I will review feedback on my work, recognise where I can improve and use each assessment as an opportunity to develop stronger learning habits.

Academic Responsibility

Learn with honesty. Submit with confidence.

I recognise that responsible academic work requires honesty, independent thinking and respect for the ideas of others. As I develop my academic skills, I will aim to:

  • Use credible sources and check the reliability of information.
  • Acknowledge sources and follow the referencing style required by my lecturers.
  • Explain ideas in my own words and distinguish them from direct quotations.
  • Follow institutional rules for collaboration and the use of digital or AI tools.
  • Ask for guidance when assignment instructions or referencing requirements are unclear.
