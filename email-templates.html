07 / Communication Toolkit

Words that work.
Communication that counts.

Professional email examples for academic communication and future career opportunities. These templates provide a starting point for communicating clearly, respectfully and confidently.

Professional standard

Clear. Respectful. Purposeful.

Every email should make its purpose easy to understand.

Use a suitable subject line, professional greeting and clear closing.
