Personal journal / 003

Learning
about myself.

Starting my journey in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics has given me an opportunity to think about who I am, what I want to achieve and the habits I need to develop. This reflection explores my learning, personal strengths, areas for improvement and the professional I hope to become.

A thought to carry forward
Growth begins when I am willing to learn from where I am today.

I do not expect to have everything figured out at the beginning of my journey. What matters is that I remain committed to improving.

In this reflection My beginning What I'm learning My strengths Areas to improve Personal growth Looking ahead
01 / THE START

A new chapter in my education.

Reflecting on where I am and what this stage of my journey means to me.

Beginning my studies in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics at Botswana School of Business Sciences represents an important step in my academic journey. As a first-year student, I understand that I am still building the foundation on which my future career will depend. I see this stage as an opportunity to explore new ideas, develop my abilities and become more confident in my potential.

I would describe myself as focused, hardworking, friendly and determined to grow professionally. These qualities influence the way I approach my goals. I want to take my education seriously while remaining willing to learn from other people and from experiences that challenge me.

Looking at my journey so far, I recognise that education is about more than passing assessments. It also involves developing discipline, learning how to manage responsibilities and understanding how my actions affect the people I work with.

02 / LEARNING

Connecting knowledge to real life.

Understanding why my programme matters beyond the classroom.

One of the reasons I am interested in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics is the connection between information and business decisions. I want to understand how organisations use information to identify problems, recognise opportunities and make informed choices.

My academic interests include business and management, as well as the practical skills needed in the workplace. I recognise that developing in this field will require me to build my understanding gradually, practise what I learn and remain curious when I encounter unfamiliar concepts.

I also understand the importance of transferable skills. Communication, teamwork, time management and the ability to organise information are valuable because professional work often involves collaborating with others and explaining ideas clearly.

I want to learn how to use knowledge, not simply memorise it.

As I continue through my programme, I want to become more comfortable applying my knowledge to practical tasks. I believe that this will help me prepare for the responsibilities of an industrial attachment and, eventually, a career in business analysis or data analysis.

03 / SELF-AWARENESS

Recognising what I bring.

Identifying the personal qualities I can continue to develop and put into practice.

I consider my determination and willingness to learn to be important strengths. When I set a goal, I want to give myself the opportunity to work towards it consistently. I also value being friendly and reliable because I believe that positive relationships and mutual respect contribute to a better learning environment.

My previous leadership roles as a Minister of Justice and a class representative are relevant to the way I think about responsibility. These roles form part of my background and remind me of the importance of representing others appropriately, communicating clearly and taking responsibilities seriously.

I want to build on these qualities throughout my studies. Being a good student and future professional requires more than confidence in my strengths; it also requires me to recognise when I need help, accept constructive feedback and remain open to different points of view.

04 / IMPROVEMENT

Being honest about my development.

Growth requires me to recognise skills that still need attention.

As a first-year student, I know that there is still a great deal for me to learn. I am at the beginning of my professional journey, so I need to keep strengthening my academic knowledge and becoming more familiar with the expectations of the workplace.

One area I want to keep developing is my practical ability to work with information and digital tools. Knowing that Microsoft Word, Excel and PowerPoint are relevant to my programme is only a starting point. I want to become more capable of using these tools appropriately for academic tasks and, in time, professional responsibilities.

I also want to continue improving my time management, communication and confidence when approaching unfamiliar tasks. Rather than viewing improvement as a sign of weakness, I want to see it as a normal part of learning.

My approach is to take my responsibilities seriously, ask questions when I do not understand something, practise consistently and use feedback to identify what I can do better. These are habits I can develop as I progress through my studies.

Turning reflection into action

The person I'm working to become.

Reflection becomes more useful when it helps me decide what to keep doing and what to improve. These are the areas I want to focus on throughout my academic journey.

01 / Academic development

Learn with intention

I want to approach my coursework with curiosity, build my understanding of key concepts and practise applying what I learn.

02 / Professional skills

Become workplace-ready

I aim to strengthen my communication, teamwork, organisation and ability to use digital tools for meaningful tasks.

03 / Personal responsibility

Build consistent habits

I want to manage my time more deliberately, take responsibility for my commitments and remain dependable when working with others.

04 / Self-development

Stay open to feedback

I want to recognise mistakes as opportunities to improve and learn from people with different knowledge and experiences.

The road ahead

My future is something I can prepare for.

I know that building a successful career will take time, commitment and practical experience. I want to use my time as a student to prepare myself for opportunities rather than wait until graduation to begin thinking about my future.

01

Complete my qualification

Continue developing my knowledge and work towards completing my Business Intelligence and Data Analytics studies.

02

Gain industrial attachment experience

Seek opportunities to observe professional practice, apply my developing skills and learn from experienced people.

03

Explore business and data analysis

Develop the skills and understanding needed to work towards opportunities in my areas of career interest.

04

Contribute professionally in Botswana

Build a career where I can continue learning, take responsibility and contribute positively to an organisation.

I am still learning, still developing and still becoming.

My journey is in progress, and I intend to make the most of every opportunity to grow.
